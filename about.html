What began as a small Newcastle record shop has grown alongside the music it loves. We’ve seen formats change and scenes come and go, but the thrill of finding the right album has never gone out of style.

Today, our team still picks every title with care. Whether you’re after a rock record to turn up loud or a pop album to soundtrack the journey, we’re here to help you find it.
