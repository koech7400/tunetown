Our History

TuneTown began in 2015 as a small stall at a weekend market, selling second-hand records and CDs to neighbours who shared a love of music. Word spread quickly, and within two years we had moved into our first shopfront.

Today TuneTown is an online store serving customers across the country. We still choose every album ourselves, and we still believe the best recommendations come from people who genuinely love what they sell.
